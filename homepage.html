K-Score Breakdown

Our unique K-Score algorithm analyzes the top 20 holders:

  • Accumulators - Increased position (150%+)
  • Holders - Maintained position (100%+)
  • Reducers - Reduced position (50-99%)
  • Extractors - Sold most (<50%)

Formula: Verification (30) + Volume (20) + Conviction (40) + MCap (10)
